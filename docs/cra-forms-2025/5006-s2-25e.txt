Schedule ON(S2) 
 
Provincial Amounts Transferred from your Spouse or Common-Law Partner 
 
Complete this schedule to claim a transfer of the unused part of your 
spouse's or common-law partner's provincial amounts below. 
 
Attach a copy of this schedule to your paper return. 
 
You can claim these amounts if you are eligible to claim the equivalent 
federal amounts on your federal Schedule 2. 
 
If your spouse or common-law partner is filing a return, use the amounts they 
entered on their Form ON428. 
 
If your spouse or common-law partner is not filing a return, use the amounts 
they would enter on their Form ON428 as if they were filing a return, and 
attach their information slips. 
 
If your spouse or common-law partner was not a resident of Ontario at the end 
of the year, special rules may apply. For more information, call the Canada 
Revenue Agency at 1-800-959-8281.
